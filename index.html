About

I want to become the person who can see the whole system—and still understand the details.

I work where product, data, and writing meet. Long term, I want to lead organizations that solve durable problems for people underserved by today’s systems.

That direction has taken me from AI app generation to developer documentation to the operating tools behind climate balloons. The domains change. The pattern does not: understand how the system really works, decide what should matter, and give a team a better way forward.

At Stanford, I studied computer science and human-computer interaction, ran a 50+ person newsroom on a 24-hour cycle, and learned that technical depth and clear storytelling are not separate skills.
